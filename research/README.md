# Research evidence

Photographs and data behind timegrapher's research pages, moved here from
`docs/research/assets/` in the timegrapher repo on 2026-11-15. The pages
themselves stay in [timegrapher's docs/research](https://github.com/peterhudson/timegrapher/tree/main/docs/research)
and link to the files here.

- `train-wheels/<calibre>/`: microscope photographs of train wheels and
  pinions with their counted teeth or leaves, the tooth-tip coordinates and
  the originals' manifest. Cited by the train-wheel count pages.
- `amplitude-validation/<movement>/<date>/`: optical amplitude comparisons
  (video-frame balance measurements against the audio reading), with the
  analysis scripts that reproduce them.
- `seiko-miyota/`: the sources gathered for the Seiko and Miyota train-period
  research.
