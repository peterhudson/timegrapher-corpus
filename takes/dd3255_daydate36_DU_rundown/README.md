# Day-Date 36 replica, Dandong 3255 movement: dial up, run-down, 67 h 29 min

Example take: it is public and can be fetched by name (`scripts/fetch.py DEST dd3255_daydate36_DU_rundown`), but `regress` and CI never read it. No checked readings are given here. The app's own last live reading, as the app showed it when the file was saved, is the `result` block at the end of `raw/app_session.json`; it is not a checked reading. The take is held out for timegrapher#1817 until a method and its acceptance are posted there. It is also the take behind timegrapher#1819 (beat error flipping in Live). Files 093 to 112 were that work's first hold-out. Files 113 onward are held out from it too, until Norm says otherwise on #1819.

- Recorded with the desktop app (timegrapher-app 1.0.0) as one take, dial up, starting 2026-11-14 05:45:12Z, letting the watch run down, until 2026-11-17 01:14:23Z. Lift 59° as set in the app, 28,800 bph. 48 kHz, 16-bit mono.
- **Complete:** 235 files (`take_part01-001` to `-235`), 242,950.4 s of sound (the app's own duration), 11,218,330,620 bytes. They were added in three batches: 001 to 092 and 093 to 112 while the take was still recording, and 113 to 235 after it stopped.
- **Calibre:** the movement is a Dandong 3255 (day and date). The app's own files in `raw/` say "Dandong 3235", because the app's calibre list offered 3255 only as an alias of the 3235.
- The audio is the app's own `audio-NNN.flac` files, copied byte for byte (each SHA-256 checked against its source). `raw/file_map.txt` gives the original names.
- `raw/app_session.json` and `raw/app_session.toml.txt` are the app's own session files. `app_session.json` is the app's final copy and lists all 235 segments. `app_session.toml.txt` holds the watch settings and lists no segments. The only change to either is the watch name, shortened to "DayDate 36". The owner's watch file is not published. `app_session.toml.txt` is the app's `session.toml` with only its name changed, so that `regress` does not read `raw/` as a session (as in `pepsi3285_DU_7h`).
- **Breaks:** the app logged 177 breaks, each with a gap of 0 s and the note "paused". All files are kept, so they join as the app recorded them.
- `clock.csv` is the app's clock log (Unix time against audio frames), copied from the app's final files.
