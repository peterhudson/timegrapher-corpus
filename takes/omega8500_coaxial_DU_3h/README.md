# Omega 8500 (co-axial escapement), dial up, 2 h 54 min

Smoke take, recorded by the corpus maintainer on a watch lent for co-axial testing; the watch's owner is not named. It is the corpus's first long co-axial take and its first Omega 8500: an automatic movement, 25,200 bph, dial up, from full wind. With `omega3861_coaxial_DU_10min` and `omega3861_coaxial_DU_10min_witschi` it gives the co-axial reading a third watch and almost three hours of drift. No reference figures: no tg reading and no paired reading from another instrument.

- **Where it came from:** recorded with the desktop app (timegrapher-app 1.1.0) on the C-Media USB pickup, from 2026-11-27 20:21:48Z to 23:16:05Z (10,456.548 s). The watch is on loan for co-axial testing; the app's watch record marks it fully wound at the start of the take. Where on the bench it sat is not recorded, so treat "dial up" as the pickup's own position, which may not be level (see the corpus README).
- **The files:** four files, `take_part01-001.flac` to `-004.flac`, the app's `audio-001.flac` to `audio-004.flac` byte for byte (`raw/file_map.txt`; SHA-256 in `corpus.json`): 2038.468, 3600.000, 3600.000 and 1218.080 s (97,846,482 + 172,800,000 + 172,800,000 + 58,467,830 = 501,914,312 frames, which is also the last frame count in `clock.csv`), 48 kHz, 16-bit mono, with no tags. Each decodes without error.
- `raw/app_session.json` and `raw/app_session.toml.txt` are the app's own session files, unchanged (the toml renamed so `regress` does not read `raw/` as a session). `clock.csv` is the app's clock log, unchanged. The app logged "paused" at 2038.47 s and at the end, and a break at 2038.47 s with `gap_s = 0`, where `audio-002.flac` starts. The app's `result` block is its last live reading (co-axial estimate, lift 38°: rate −2.6 s/d, amplitude 282°); it is not a checked reading.
- **Lift angle:** 38°, from the timegrapher calibre table's Omega 8500 row (`lift_known = true`). Any amplitude read from this take is a co-axial estimate (beta).
- **Escapement:** `session.toml` says `escapement = "co-axial"` and names the calibre, which the table also marks co-axial. On a co-axial, beat error comes from the unlock only.
- **Holdout rule:** a maintainer's bench take, not a contributed recording, so the rule does not apply; for the record, `scripts/holdout_rule.py` on it says development (first byte 0x5e).
- **Privacy check:** a spectrogram of the whole take shows the ticks and steady tones, with no bursts like speech. It was not listened to by ear at import.
